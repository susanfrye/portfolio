About

Ecologist, spatial-data specialist, R & Shiny developer

I hold a PhD in ecology with a focus on landscape-scale forest dynamics and long-term biodiversity monitoring — the kind of complex, multi-site datasets that demand both rigorous statistics and clear communication.

Over a decade of field and analytical work has made me a specialist in spatial data, from plot-level inventory to province-wide habitat mapping using QGIS, R spatial packages, and remote sensing products.

I build R and Shiny tools that turn raw ecological data into decisions: dashboards for conservation authorities, interactive reports for forestry clients, and monitoring frameworks for municipalities across Ontario.
