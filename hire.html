Where I can plug into your team

Pollinator & Native-Bee Surveys

My specialty. Targeted native-bee and pollinator surveys with expert hand-netting, pan/vane trapping, and voucher-quality specimen handling. Protocol design or execution to your study plan — I can run the pollinator component your firm doesn't staff in-house.

  • Field collection & curation
  • Expert-level bee identification
  • Protocol design on request

Vegetation Plots & ELC Field Support

Extra hands for vegetation sampling and Ecological Land Classification fieldwork during peak season. Quadrat and transect plots, cover estimates, and plant ID recorded to your data standards so it drops straight into your workflow.

  • Quadrat & transect sampling
  • ELC community mapping support
  • Clean, standardized field data

Insect & Bycatch Identification

Lab-based sorting and identification of insect samples and trap bycatch. Whether it's pollinator pan-trap contents or mixed arthropod bycatch from another program, I'll sort, pin, and identify to the level your project requires and return organized, database-ready records.

  • Sorting, pinning & curation
  • ID to project-required resolution
  • Database-ready specimen records

Report Drafting From Your Field Data

Fully remote. Hand me your raw field data and I'll draft the methods, results, and discussion in your template and voice — clean, technically sound, and ready for your senior review. Ideal for clearing a reporting backlog while your field crews stay in the field.

  • Methods, results & discussion
  • Written to your firm's template
  • Fully remote turnaround

Statistical Analysis & Figures in R

Reproducible analysis and publication-quality figures in R — community composition, diversity metrics, GLMs and mixed models, and clean ggplot graphics. You get the outputs plus documented, re-runnable code your team can reuse.

  • Diversity & community analysis
  • GLM / mixed-model statistics
  • Documented, reproducible code
