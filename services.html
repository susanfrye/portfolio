Services

Analysis, dashboards, and maps for environmental work

End-to-end analytical and visualization services tailored to environmental and forestry consulting firms (as overflow / subcontract capacity), conservation authorities, municipalities, and environmental NGOs.

Statistical Analysis & Reporting

Rigorous environmental and ecological statistics — species and distribution reports, biodiversity and diversity indices, mixed models, and trend analysis — delivered as reproducible R Markdown or Quarto reports ready for regulators or boards.

  • Species & biodiversity reporting
  • Distribution & occurrence analysis
  • Long-term monitoring trend reports
  • Regulatory-ready, reproducible workflows

Interactive Dashboards & Data Tools

Interactive dashboards and tools that let your team explore, filter, and present data — no coding required. From field-data portals to executive reporting.

  • Interactive maps & charts
  • Filterable data tables
  • Branded, client-ready UI
  • Hosted & deployment support

Spatial Mapping & Monitoring Design

QGIS-based spatial analysis and map production, plus statistically grounded monitoring program design — plot layouts, sample size calculations, and indicator selection.

  • Habitat mapping & classification
  • Monitoring program design
  • Print & web-ready cartography
  • Sample design & power analysis

How I work
